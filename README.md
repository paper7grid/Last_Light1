# Last_Light1
Hello! This is a 3D horror game I made using the Godot game engine. Please go full screen for best experience, I had to scale down a bit! thanks. The setting of the story is that: You are kidnapped and are left clues to escape out of the house. The killer has set a trap with a few clues around the house in the form of notes. If you are smart enough to solve his riddles and escape within 15 minutes you are truly a survivor. BUT if the time is up and you were not able to solve them, then you die, the killer kills you. Show your survival skills and escape!!

Some of the controls: 

F- Flashlight

P - to pause/unpause the game (and to open menu)

E - to interact (pickup or open door) 

Space - to jump (please jump to move up the stairs!)

There are X buttons on the top left of each note to close them!

Arrow keys and mouse to look around/ move. 

Have fun playing the game!! 
You can play it at itch.io: https://paper78grid.itch.io/last-light
